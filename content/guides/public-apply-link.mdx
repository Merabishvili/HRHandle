title: Public apply link
summary: Share a branded application form for any open vacancy.
updated: 2026-05-21
---

Every vacancy can have a public application form on its own URL. Share the link on LinkedIn, a job board, or your own website — candidates apply directly, no HRHandle account required.

## Activate the form

Open a vacancy and switch to the **Apply Link** tab. If the form is not yet active, click **Activate Application Form**.

## Copy and share the link

Once active, the tab shows the public URL and a short summary of what candidates will see. Use the icons next to the URL to copy it or open it in a new tab.

<Screenshot
  src="/guide/screenshots/public-apply-link-active.png"
  alt="The Apply Link tab in active state with the copy and open icons highlighted"
  caption="Copy the link with the left icon, or preview it in a new tab with the right icon."
/>

The link has the shape `https://hrhandle.com/apply/<token>` and works until the form is deactivated.

## What candidates see

Candidates land on a branded page that includes the vacancy description, responsibilities, and requirements. They upload a CV first — HRHandle parses it and pre-fills the personal details — then review and submit.

<Screenshot
  src="/guide/screenshots/public-apply-link-public-form.png"
  alt="The public apply page showing the CV upload area at the top and personal detail fields below"
  caption="CV upload sits at the top; HRHandle's AI extracts name, email, phone, and LinkedIn from the file."
/>

The candidate-facing form asks for:

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| CV (PDF or Word, up to 10 MB) | Yes |
| First name | Yes |
| Last name | Yes |
| Email | Yes |
| Phone | No |
| LinkedIn URL | No |

## After submission

When a candidate submits the form:

1. A candidate record is created in your organization's talent pool.
2. An application is created in the pipeline at the **Applied** stage.
3. Owners and admins receive an in-app notification.
4. The applicant receives a confirmation email.

From there, work the candidate through the [pipeline](/guide/pipeline-kanban) or [schedule an interview](/guide/schedule-interview).

## Deactivating the form

To stop accepting new applications, open the **Apply Link** tab and click **Deactivate Form**. The link stops working immediately. Existing candidates and applications are kept; only new submissions are blocked.
