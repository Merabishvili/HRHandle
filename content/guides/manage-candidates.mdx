The Candidates section is your central talent pool. Every applicant — whether added by hand, parsed from a CV, or submitted through the public apply form — appears here.

## Open the candidates list

From the sidebar, click **Candidates**. You will see the list of every candidate in your organization, with search, filters, and an **Add Candidate** action.

## Choose how to add the candidate

HRHandle offers two paths so you don't waste time on data entry when the CV already has everything.

<Screenshot
  src="/guide/screenshots/manage-candidates-entry-mode.png"
  alt="The Add Candidate page showing two options: Upload CV first or Fill manually"
  caption="Upload CV first to auto-fill the form; Fill manually if there is no CV yet."
/>

- **Upload CV first** — Upload a PDF or Word file. HRHandle parses it with AI and pre-fills name, email, phone, LinkedIn URL, work history, and education. Review and submit.
- **Fill manually** — Open the empty form and type details by hand. You can still attach a CV as a document later.

Either path saves to the same candidate record.

## View and edit a candidate

Click any row in the list to open the candidate detail page. From there you can:

- Edit personal details and contact information
- Add or remove work experience and education entries
- Attach documents (CVs, cover letters, portfolios)
- Add internal notes
- Change the candidate's general status (Active, Hired, Archived)
- See every application that links them to a vacancy

## Search and filter the list

The top of the candidates list has a search box and status tabs.

- **Search** matches against first name, last name, and email.
- **Status tabs** filter by Active, Hired, or Archived.
- **Columns** lets you choose which fields appear in the table — useful if you store custom data like preferred languages or notice period.

## General status vs. application status

These are two different things:

| Status type | Where it lives | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **General status** | On the candidate | Their relationship with your organization — Active, Hired, Archived. |
| **Application status** | On each application (candidate ↔ vacancy) | Where they are in a specific hiring pipeline — Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer, Hired, Rejected. |

When you move an application to **Hired**, the candidate's general status automatically syncs to **Hired** as well. Reverting the application reverts the candidate.

## Export and bulk actions

The list supports CSV export of every candidate visible after filters and search — useful for offline reporting or compliance. Click the **Export CSV** button next to **Add Candidate**.
