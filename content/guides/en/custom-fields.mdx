Custom fields let you keep information HRHandle doesn't ask for by default — a referral, a budget approval, a priority. Owners and admins create them in **Settings → Custom fields**, for candidates and for vacancies, up to 20 of each.

## Fields and groups

<Screenshot
  name="custom-fields"
  alt="The Custom fields settings with the Candidates and Vacancies tabs, the field count, a group of fields and the Add Group button highlighted"
  caption="Fields are organised in groups; each group is a section on the form."
/>

1. **Candidates** / **Vacancies** — whose fields you're editing.
2. **Fields used** — how many of the 20 fields you've created.
3. **A group** — click its name to open it. It lists the fields with their type; the bin deletes a field or the whole group. Deleting a field also deletes everything saved in it.
4. **Add Group** — type a name, for example "Recruiting details", and click **Add Group**.

## Add a field

Open a group and click **Add field**.

<Screenshot
  name="custom-field-add"
  alt="The new field form with a name, the Dropdown type and three options"
  caption="A dropdown field with three options."
/>

Type the **Field Name** and choose its **Type**:

| Type | Holds |
|---|---|
| Short text (100 chars) | A name, a code, a short note. |
| Long text (5000 chars) | A longer description. |
| Number | A figure, such as years or a budget. |
| Date | A date picked from a calendar. |
| Dropdown | One of the **Options** you list, separated by commas. |
| Yes / No | A tick box. |

Tick **Mark as required** if the field must always be filled in, then click **Add Field**.

## Where custom fields appear

- **Vacancy fields** — in step 2 of the vacancy wizard and on the vacancy's **Job description** tab (see [Create a vacancy](/guide/post-a-vacancy)).
- **Candidate fields** — when you add a candidate and under **Details** on the candidate profile (see [The candidate profile](/guide/candidate-profile)).
- **In lists** — add any custom field as a column in the candidate or vacancy list (see [Search and filters](/guide/search-and-filters)).
