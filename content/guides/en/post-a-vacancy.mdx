A vacancy holds everything about one open role: its description, the interview scorecard, the questions candidates answer when they apply, and every application you receive. You create it in a five-step wizard. Only the position title is required, so you can fill in the rest now or come back to it later.

## Open the wizard

From the sidebar, click **Vacancies**, then click **Create vacancy** in the top-right corner.

<Screenshot
  name="vacancies-list"
  alt="The Vacancies list with the Create vacancy button highlighted"
  caption="The Vacancies list. Create vacancy (1) opens the wizard."
/>

Anyone on your team can create vacancies. Every vacancy that isn't archived counts toward your plan's vacancy limit — see [Plans and billing](/guide/billing).

## Step 1 — Basics

<Screenshot
  name="create-vacancy-basics"
  alt="Step 1 of the Create vacancy wizard with the position title and the other basics filled in"
  caption="Step 1 — Basics. Only the position title is required."
/>

1. **The steps.** The current step is highlighted and finished steps get a check mark. Click **Back** to return to an earlier step — nothing you've entered is lost.
2. **Position title** — the only required field. Candidates see it on the apply form and on your jobs page.
3. **Next** takes you to the next step. From step 2 on, you can also click **Save as draft** at any time.

The other fields are optional:

- **Department** and **Location** — shown to candidates.
- **Sector** — for filters and reports.
- **Work mode** — on-site, hybrid or remote.
- **Employment type** (full-time, part-time, contract or internship) — shown to candidates. **Openings** — how many people you want to hire.
- **Hiring manager** — the teammate responsible for this role. You can pick anyone on your team.

## Step 2 — Dates & compensation

<Screenshot
  name="create-vacancy-dates"
  alt="Step 2 of the wizard with start and end dates, a salary range and the organization's custom fields"
  caption="Step 2 — Dates & compensation, with your organization's own fields at the bottom."
/>

1. **Start date** and **End date.** If you leave the start date empty, it becomes the day you create the vacancy. The night after the end date, HRHandle closes the vacancy automatically.
2. **Minimum salary**, **Maximum salary** and **Currency** — all optional. Only your team sees the salary range; it isn't shown to candidates. The currency starts as your organization's local currency.
3. **Your custom fields.** If your organization has added its own fields for vacancies, they appear here. Set them up in [Custom fields](/guide/custom-fields).

## Step 3 — Description & AI

Describe the role in three fields: **About the Job** (required to publish), **Responsibilities** and **Requirements**. Candidates see all three on the apply form and on your jobs page, and they're included when you share the vacancy on LinkedIn.

### Let AI draft the description

Open **AI assist — suggest job description sections** to get a first draft based on what you entered in steps 1 and 2.

<Screenshot
  name="create-vacancy-ai"
  alt="The AI assist panel with drafts for About the job, Responsibilities and Requirements"
  caption="The AI writes a draft for each section. Nothing in your form changes until you apply it."
/>

1. **Optional context for the AI** — add what the AI can't know: team size, tools, office days.
2. **Generate**, next to each section, writes a draft. Click **Regenerate** for a different version, or **Copy** to take just that section.
3. **Apply all to form** puts the drafts into the fields below. Then edit them like any other text.

The AI writes in your organization's content language (see [Organization settings](/guide/organization-settings)), and its drafts are only suggestions — always read them before you publish.

### Check the wording, then choose visibility

<Screenshot
  name="create-vacancy-description"
  alt="Step 3 with the description filled in, the two AI assist panels and the Show on public jobs page switch turned on"
  caption="Step 3 — the finished description, ready for your jobs page."
/>

1. **AI assist — suggest job description sections** — the panel from the previous picture, folded away.
2. **AI assist — check inclusive language** — click **Run check** to look for phrases that may put some candidates off. It suggests alternatives but never changes your text.
3. **Show on public jobs page** — lists the vacancy on your organization's jobs page and creates its apply link. If you leave it off, you can turn the apply link on later in the vacancy's **Apply form** tab.

## Step 4 — Scorecard & questions

<Screenshot
  name="create-vacancy-scorecard"
  alt="Step 4 with four scorecard attributes and two screening questions set as knockouts"
  caption="Step 4 — what interviewers rate, and what applicants answer."
/>

1. **Interview scorecard** — the skills and qualities interviewers rate from 1 to 5 after an interview. Type an attribute and press Enter, or click **Suggest from JD** to let the AI propose some from your description. Click the star to switch an attribute between **Must-have** and **Nice-to-have**.
2. **Screening questions** — questions applicants answer on the apply form. Choose the answer type (**Yes / No**, **Short text**, **Number** or **Select**) and click **Add**. Each question is **Informational** or a **Knockout**. For a knockout, set the passing answer — for example "Yes", or a number of at least 2. Applicants who answer differently can still apply: their application is flagged, and you decide. Short-text questions can only be informational.

You can skip this step. The vacancy then gets a default scorecard and no screening questions, and you can change both later on the vacancy page. More in [Assessments and scorecards](/guide/assessments-and-scorecards).

## Step 5 — Review & publish

<Screenshot
  name="create-vacancy-review"
  alt="The review step summarizing the vacancy, with Publish now selected"
  caption="Step 5 — check everything, then publish."
/>

The review shows everything you entered. Click **Edit** on any section to jump back to that step.

1. **How do you want to finish?** — **Publish now** opens the vacancy for applications. **Save as draft** keeps it hidden until you're ready.
2. The button at the bottom follows your choice: **Publish now** or **Save as draft**.

To publish, **About the Job** must be filled in; a draft only needs a title.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Draft | Only your team can see the vacancy. It has no apply link yet. |
| Open | The vacancy accepts applications. With **Show on public jobs page** on, it's listed on your jobs page and has an apply link. |

You can change the status at any time on the vacancy page — including **On hold**, **Closed** and **Archived**. See [The vacancy page](/guide/vacancy-page).

## What happens next

After you publish, the new vacancy's page opens.

<Screenshot
  name="create-vacancy-done"
  alt="The new vacancy's page with the Copy apply link and View pipeline buttons and the tabs highlighted"
  caption="The new vacancy, open and ready for candidates."
/>

1. **Copy apply link** — share the link anywhere: job boards, social media, your website or a message.
2. **View pipeline →** — the vacancy's own board, where you move candidates from stage to stage.
3. **The tabs** — everything you set in the wizard can be changed here: the job description, the scorecard, the apply form and the vacancy's settings.

Behind the scenes, HRHandle also:

1. Gives the vacancy its own pipeline, copied from your **Main pipeline** (Settings → Main pipeline).
2. Adds your screening questions to the apply form.
3. Shows on the **Overview** tab how long the vacancy has been open and how many candidates are active.

From here, [share the vacancy](/guide/share-a-vacancy), [add candidates](/guide/add-candidates) or open [the pipeline](/guide/pipeline).
