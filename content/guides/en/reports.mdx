Reports show how your hiring is going: how candidates move through the stages, how long hiring takes and which sources bring you hires. Open **Reports** in the sidebar. Everyone in your team can see them.

## Pipeline conversion

<Screenshot
  name="reports-pipeline"
  alt="The Pipeline report with the report tabs, the period, the summary, the funnel and the stage-to-stage conversion highlighted"
  caption="How far applications get, stage by stage."
/>

1. **Report tabs** — **Pipeline**, **Time to hire** and **Sources**.
2. **Period** — **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** (the default), **Last 90 days**, **Last 365 days** or **All time**. Every report counts the applications made in that period.
3. **Summary** — the number of applications, how many reached the interview stage, how many were hired, and how many were rejected or withdrew.
4. **Funnel** — how many applications reached each stage. A candidate who was rejected after an interview still counts for the stages they reached.
5. **Stage-to-stage conversion** — the share of applications that moved on from each stage to the next.

## Time to hire

<Screenshot
  name="reports-time-to-hire"
  alt="The Time to hire report with the summary and the table by vacancy highlighted"
  caption="How many days it takes from application to hire."
/>

1. **Summary** — the number of hires and the days from application to hire: the **Median**, the middle half of hires (**25th–75th**) and the **Mean**.
2. **By vacancy** — the hires and the median time to hire for each vacancy.

## Sources

<Screenshot
  name="reports-sources"
  alt="The Sources report with the summary and the chart of where hires come from highlighted"
  caption="Which channels bring applications — and which bring hires."
/>

1. **Summary** — applications, hires and the overall conversion from application to hire.
2. **Sources · where hires come from** — applications and hires for each source: the public apply form, LinkedIn, candidates added by hand and imports. The **By source** table under it has the exact numbers and the conversion for each source.

To get more applications through your apply link, see [Share a vacancy](/guide/share-a-vacancy).
