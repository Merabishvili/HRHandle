Every HRHandle screen has the same frame: the sidebar on the left and the header along the top. This guide shows what each part does, so you always know where to find things.

## The main screen

When you sign in, you land on the **Pipeline**: every candidate across all your open vacancies, arranged by hiring stage.

<Screenshot
  name="nav-overview"
  alt="The Pipeline screen with the sidebar, the header controls and the plan box highlighted"
  caption="The sidebar and the header are on every screen."
/>

1. **The sidebar** — the main sections of HRHandle (see the table below).
2. **Search…** — finds candidates, vacancies and notes from anywhere. Shortcut: Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) or the / key. See [Search and filters](/guide/search-and-filters).
3. **Help** — opens this guide in a new tab.
4. **Notifications** — a red number shows how many are unread.
5. **Your account** — your initials. Opens the account menu.
6. **Plan** — the plan your organization is on. During the trial, a pill in the header also shows how many days are left.

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Pipeline** | Every candidate in every open vacancy, by stage. See [The pipeline](/guide/pipeline). |
| **Vacancies** | Your job openings: create, edit and share them. See [Create a vacancy](/guide/post-a-vacancy). |
| **Candidates** | Everyone who ever applied or was added — your talent pool. See [The candidate profile](/guide/candidate-profile). |
| **Interviews** | Upcoming and past interviews. See [Schedule interviews](/guide/schedule-interview). |
| **Reports** | Pipeline conversion, candidate sources and time to hire. See [Reports](/guide/reports). |
| **Settings** | Your profile and your organization's settings. Which ones you see depends on your role. |
| **Support** | Send a message to the HRHandle team. |

On a phone or a small screen, the sidebar folds into a menu button in the top-left corner.

## Notifications

Click the bell to see what happened while you were away.

<Screenshot
  name="nav-notifications"
  alt="The notifications panel with new applications, a mention and a scheduled interview"
  caption="Unread notifications have a blue dot."
/>

HRHandle notifies you about new applications, @mentions in notes, scheduled interviews, offer answers, hires and more. Click a notification to open it, or click **Mark all read**. You choose which ones you get — and whether they also come by email — in **Settings → Notifications**. See [Your profile and security](/guide/profile-and-security).

## Your account menu

Click your initials in the top-right corner.

<Screenshot
  name="nav-account-menu"
  alt="The account menu with Profile, Settings, Billing and Sign out"
  caption="Your name and email, and shortcuts to your settings."
/>

- **Profile** — your name, photo and interface language.
- **Settings** — all the settings you have access to.
- **Billing** — your organization's plan and payments.
- **Sign out** — signs you out on this device.

## Change the interface language

Each person chooses their own interface language — English, Georgian or Russian — in **Settings → Profile → Language**. It only changes what you see. The language candidates see is set for the whole organization in [Organization settings](/guide/organization-settings).
