Click a candidate's name anywhere — on the pipeline, in the candidate list or on a vacancy — to open their profile. It brings together everything about the person: their applications, what to do next, the team's assessments, their background and documents, and the full history of what happened. **Previous candidate** and **Next candidate** move through the list without going back.

## The top of the profile

<Screenshot
  name="candidate-profile"
  alt="A candidate profile with the header actions, active application, stage card, Actions and Documents highlighted"
  caption="Who the candidate is, where they are, and what to do next."
/>

1. **Header actions** — **Add to Vacancy** puts the candidate into another vacancy's pipeline. **Edit** changes their personal details. **⋯** holds **Merge candidates** and **Delete candidate**.
2. **Active application** — if the candidate is in more than one vacancy, choose which one you're looking at. Closed applications are kept in the history.
3. **The stage card** — where the candidate is (Applied → Screening → Interview → Offer → Hired) and what this stage needs from you. At the interview stage, for example, it shows the interview with a **Join** link, your scorecard, **Edit scorecard** and **Reschedule**. At the offer stage, it's where you write the offer.
4. **Actions** — move the candidate on (for example **Advance to Offer**), **Schedule** an interview, send an **Email** or **Reject** them.
5. **Documents** — CVs, cover letters and other files (PDF or Word, up to 10 MB). Choose the document type, click **Upload**, and open or delete files from the list.

Further down are the **Assessments** — every reviewer's scorecard with their score and recommendation (see [Assessments and scorecards](/guide/assessments-and-scorecards)) — and the candidate's **Experience** and **Education**, which you can add to or edit. On the right, **Contact** has the email, phone and LinkedIn with copy buttons, and **Details** has the salary expectation, notice period, location, languages, source and the date they were added, together with your organization's own fields.

The status next to the name is set automatically:

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| Active | The candidate has at least one open application. |
| Inactive | No open applications — for example after an import, or when every application has ended. |
| Hired | The candidate was hired for a vacancy. |

## Notes and activity

The **Activity** feed shows everything that happened with the candidate, newest first.

<Screenshot
  name="candidate-activity"
  alt="The Activity feed with a note that mentions a teammate and the note box at the bottom"
  caption="Notes, interviews, stage changes, offers and documents in one timeline."
/>

1. **A note with an @mention** — notes are visible to your whole team. Mentioning someone sends them a notification.
2. **The note box** — write a note and press the send button. Type @ and choose a teammate to mention them.

Use the filters above the feed — **Notes**, **Interviews**, **Stage changes**, **Offers**, **Documents** — to see just one kind of event.

## Merge duplicates

When the same person ended up in HRHandle twice — for example they applied again with a personal email — merge the two profiles. Click **⋯** → **Merge candidates**.

<Screenshot
  name="candidate-merge"
  alt="The Merge candidates dialog with the duplicate profile found by name"
  caption="Step 1 of 3: find the duplicate by name or email."
/>

1. **Pick the duplicate** — search by name or email; profiles with the same email are listed first.
2. **Resolve conflicts** — where the two profiles differ, choose which value to keep.
3. **Confirm** — the duplicate's applications, notes, documents, interviews and assessments move to this profile, and the duplicate disappears.

Changed your mind? For 30 days, the profile shows the merge with a **Split back** button that separates the two again.

## Delete a candidate

**⋯** → **Delete candidate** removes the candidate together with their applications. Owners and admins can restore them from the Trash within 30 days — see [Audit log and trash](/guide/audit-log-and-trash).
