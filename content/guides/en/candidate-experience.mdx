Candidates never need an account. Everything they see — your careers page, the apply form, their emails, their status page and their offer — is in your organization's language (see [Organization settings](/guide/organization-settings)) and shows them only their own application.

## Applying

Candidates find your open vacancies on your careers page and apply through a vacancy's apply link — see [Share a vacancy](/guide/share-a-vacancy). After sending the form they see a thank-you message, and they get a confirmation email. Both have a **Track your application** link to their status page.

## The status page

The status page shows how far the candidate has got, in a few simple steps that don't reveal your own stage names.

<Screenshot
  name="candidate-status"
  alt="A candidate's status page with the progress steps, an offer to review, the current status and the withdraw option highlighted"
  caption="The status page of a candidate who has received an offer."
/>

1. **Progress** — **Applied**, **In review**, **Interview** and **Decision**.
2. **The offer** — once you've sent an offer, a link to it appears here.
3. **Current status** — a short explanation and the date it last changed.
4. **Withdraw application** — the candidate can withdraw at any point until they're hired or the application is closed.

| Your stage | What the candidate sees |
|---|---|
| Applied | **Applied** — the application was received. |
| Screening and other review stages | **In review** — a recruiter is reviewing the application. |
| Interview | **Interview** — the application is at the interview stage. |
| Offer, Hired | **Decision** — the team is making, or has made, a decision and the recruiter will be in touch. |
| Rejected, Withdrawn | **Closed** |

## When a candidate withdraws

<Screenshot
  name="candidate-withdraw"
  alt="The dialog for withdrawing an application, with an optional reason"
  caption="Withdrawing takes one confirmation."
/>

The candidate confirms and can add a short reason. The application moves to **Withdrawn**, any open offer is cancelled, and the owner and admins get a notification with the candidate's name, the vacancy and the reason. The reason also appears in the candidate's activity and in the audit log. The candidate can't undo it from the status page.

1. **Closed** — the page never says "rejected". If you sent a rejection email, that's where the candidate reads your message.

## Emails candidates get

| Email | When it's sent |
|---|---|
| Application received | Always, right after someone applies through an apply link. |
| Interview invitation | When you schedule an interview with **Email candidate** ticked. |
| Status: Under review, Status: Interview | When you move the candidate forward to Screening or Interview — only if you've switched these emails on. |
| Rejection | When you reject a candidate with **Send rejection email** on. |
| Offer sent | When you click **Save & send** on an offer. |

You can change the text of each email — see [Email templates](/guide/candidate-emails).
