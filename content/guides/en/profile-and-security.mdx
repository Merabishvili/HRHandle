Your personal settings — **Profile**, **Notifications** and **Security** — only affect your own account. Open them from **Settings** in the sidebar, or from your initials in the top-right corner.

## Your profile

Open **Settings → Profile**.

<Screenshot
  name="profile-settings"
  alt="The Profile settings with the photo, Full name and Language fields highlighted"
  caption="Your name, photo and interface language."
/>

1. **Change photo** — a JPG, PNG or WebP picture of up to 2 MB. Teammates see it next to your name.
2. **Full name** — how your teammates see you, for example in notes, @mentions and the hiring team of a vacancy.
3. **Language** — the language of HRHandle's interface for you: English, Georgian or Russian. It doesn't change what candidates or your teammates see.

**Phone** is optional. Click **Save changes** when you're done. The **Account** box below shows your email, your role and when you joined; only an owner or admin can change a role.

## Password, two-factor authentication and sessions

Open **Settings → Security**.

<Screenshot
  name="security-settings"
  alt="The Security settings with the Password, Two-factor authentication and Active sessions cards highlighted"
  caption="Keep your account safe."
/>

1. **Password** — enter your **Current password**, a **New password** of at least 8 characters and **Confirm new password**, then click **Update password**. You stay signed in on this device. If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, there's no HRHandle password to change.
2. **Two-factor authentication** — adds a one-time code to every sign-in. Click **Enable 2FA**, scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy or similar), enter the 6-digit code and click **Verify and enable**. Then create your **Recovery codes** and keep them somewhere safe: each one lets you sign in once if you lose your phone.
3. **Active sessions** — every device signed in to your account. Sign a single device out, or use **Sign out everywhere** if you think someone else has access.

Your organization's owner can make two-factor authentication mandatory — see [Organization settings](/guide/organization-settings). If you lose both your phone and your recovery codes, ask an owner or admin to reset your 2FA from **Settings → Team**.

## Forgot your password?

On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**, enter your email and open the link HRHandle sends you. If you use two-factor authentication, you'll also be asked for a code before you set the new password.

## Notification settings

Open **Settings → Notifications** to choose how HRHandle tells you about each event.

<Screenshot
  name="notification-settings"
  alt="The notification settings table with In-app, Email and Slack columns for each event"
  caption="One row per event, one column per channel."
/>

| Event | When it happens |
|---|---|
| New application | Someone applies through an apply link (owner and admins). |
| Stage change | A candidate is hired or withdraws their application (owner and admins). |
| Interview reminder | An interview is scheduled with you, and the day before it. |
| Offer response | A candidate accepts or declines an offer (owner and admins). |
| @mention in a note | A teammate mentions you in a note. |

Tick **In-app** for the bell in the header and **Email** for an email. In-app @mentions are always on. What your team's Slack or Microsoft Teams channel receives is set once for the whole organization — see [Notifications, Slack and Teams](/guide/notifications). Click **Save preferences** to keep your changes.
