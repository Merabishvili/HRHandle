Add a candidate yourself when you find someone outside your apply link: a referral, a LinkedIn contact, a CV someone emailed you. People who apply through your apply link are added automatically — see [Share a vacancy](/guide/share-a-vacancy). To add many people at once, see [Import candidates](/guide/import-candidates).

## Open the form

From the sidebar, click **Candidates**, then **Add candidate**. You'll find the same button on the **Pipeline** too.

<Screenshot
  name="candidates-add-button"

/>

## Step 1 — Choose how to add the candidate

<Screenshot
  name="add-candidate-path"
  alt="The first step of Add candidate with the Upload CV first and Fill manually options"
  caption="Let the AI read the CV, or type the details yourself."
/>

1. **Upload CV first** — the AI reads the CV and fills in the form; you only check it.
2. **Fill manually** — enter the details yourself. You can still attach a CV to the candidate later.

Then click **Next: Personal**.

## Step 2 — Personal details

<Screenshot
  name="add-candidate-personal"
  alt="The Personal step with the uploaded CV and the fields the AI filled in"
  caption="The AI filled in the form from the CV."
/>

1. **The CV** — a PDF or Word file of up to 10 MB. Fields the AI filled in are marked **AI-filled · review**. The × removes the file.
2. **First name** and **Last name** (required), then email, phone, current position and company, location, timezone, salary expectation, notice period, languages and LinkedIn.

The AI is fast, but it can make mistakes — check every field it filled in before you continue.

## Step 3 — Experience & education

<Screenshot
  name="add-candidate-background"
  alt="The Experience & education step with two jobs and one degree read from the CV"
  caption="Work history and education, read from the CV."
/>

Add, change or remove jobs and degrees here. With **Fill manually**, you add them yourself.

## Step 4 — Application & notes

<Screenshot
  name="add-candidate-application"
  alt="The Application & notes step with the initial vacancy, starting stage and source highlighted"
  caption="Where the candidate starts in your hiring process."
/>

Your organization's own fields for candidates appear at the top (see [Custom fields](/guide/custom-fields)).

1. **Initial vacancy** — adds the candidate straight into a vacancy's pipeline. Leave it empty to add them to your candidate database only.
2. **Starting stage** — **Applied** by default. Choose a later stage for someone you've already spoken to.
3. **Source** — where the candidate came from, for example LinkedIn or a referral. It feeds the [Sources report](/guide/reports).

Below, **Notes** lets you add a first note about the candidate.

## Step 5 — Review and save

<Screenshot
  name="add-candidate-review"
  alt="The Review step summarizing the new candidate, with the Add candidate and Save & add another buttons"
  caption="Check everything, then save."
/>

1. **Add candidate** — saves the candidate and opens their profile.
2. **Save & add another** — saves and starts a fresh form, for when you're adding several people.

HRHandle attaches the CV to the candidate's **Documents** and, if you chose a vacancy, adds them to its pipeline at the stage you picked. A candidate can be active in up to 5 vacancies at a time, and every candidate counts toward your plan's candidate limit. Next, see [The candidate profile](/guide/candidate-profile).
