The audit log records what happened in your organization and who did it. The trash keeps deleted candidates and vacancies for 30 days, so a mistake can be undone. Both are for owners and admins, in **Settings**.

## The audit log

Open **Settings → Audit log**.

<Screenshot
  name="audit-log"
  alt="The Audit log with the Export CSV button, the filters and the list of events highlighted"
  caption="Every important action, newest first."
/>

1. **Export CSV** — download the log as a spreadsheet, with your current filters applied.
2. **Filters** — narrow the log by **Action**, **Entity type**, **User** and date (**From**, **To**), then click **Apply**.
3. **The events** — **When** it happened, **Who** did it, the **Action**, the record it concerns (**Entity**) and the **Details**, such as the stage a candidate moved from and to.

The log includes stage changes, interviews, offers, deletions and restores, imports, AI use, two-factor authentication changes, connected accounts and payments. Nobody can edit or delete it.

## The trash

When someone deletes a candidate or a vacancy, it goes to **Settings → Trash**.

<Screenshot
  name="trash"
  alt="The Trash with the Candidates and Vacancies tabs, a deleted candidate and the Restore button highlighted"
  caption="Deleted candidates and vacancies wait here for 30 days."
/>

1. **Candidates** / **Vacancies** — what's in the trash, with how many of each.
2. **A deleted item** — when it was deleted and how many days are left before it's removed for good.
3. **Restore** brings it back exactly as it was; for a candidate, their applications come back too. **Delete now** removes it permanently straight away, after you confirm.

After 30 days, items in the trash are deleted permanently and can't be restored.
