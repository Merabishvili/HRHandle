Every organization starts with a free 7-day trial. When you're ready, choose a plan and pay by card, Google Pay or Apple Pay. Owners and admins manage the plan: click your initials in the top-right corner and choose **Billing**, or open **Settings → Billing**. During the trial, the trial pill in the header opens it too.

1. **Current plan** — your plan, its price and status, and how many vacancies and candidates you have out of your plan's limit.
2. **Monthly** / **Annual** — see the prices per month when you pay monthly or for a year in advance (cheaper). Next to it you can change the currency you pay in — lari, euro or US dollar.
3. **The plans** — click **Upgrade** on a plan to pay for it. You're taken to a secure payment page and, once the payment goes through, the new plan starts straight away.
4. **Payment method** — whether a card is linked. Payments are handled securely by Flitt.

## Renewal, cancelling and refunds

Plans renew automatically at the end of each period. To stop the renewal, click **Cancel subscription** — you keep your plan until the end of the period you've paid for.

You can ask for a refund within 7 days of a payment. Approved refunds are sent within 14 business days, minus payment processing fees. If you bought a plan during your trial and get a full refund, your organization goes back to its trial for the days that are left. All the details are in our [Refund Policy](/refund).

## When the trial or plan ends

If the trial ends, or a paid plan ends without being renewed, HRHandle is paused until you choose a plan. Your data is kept. Owners and admins see the Billing page and can pay straight away; other teammates are asked to contact them. If a renewal payment fails, your plan stays available for 3 more days, so you have time to pay again.
