Every organization starts with a free 7-day trial. When you're ready, choose a plan and pay by card, Google Pay or Apple Pay. Owners and admins manage the plan: click your initials in the top-right corner and choose **Billing**, or open **Settings → Billing**. During the trial, the trial pill in the header opens it too.

## Your plan

1. **Current plan** — your plan, its price and status, and how many vacancies and candidates you have out of your plan's limit.
2. **Monthly** / **Annual** — see the prices per month when you pay monthly or for a year in advance (cheaper). Next to it you can change the currency you pay in — lari, euro or US dollar. Residents of Georgia pay in lari only.
3. **The plans** — click **Upgrade** on a plan to pay for it. A short window shows the final price and how renewal works, and asks you to tick that you agree to the Terms, the Refund Policy and automatic renewal; the first time it also asks one question (see below). You're taken to a secure payment page and, once the payment goes through, the new plan starts straight away.
4. **Payment method** — whether a card is linked. Payments are handled securely by Flitt.

1. **I am a resident of Georgia (an individual or a legal entity)** — choose **Yes** or **No**. Georgian law lets residents of Georgia pay in lari only; if you choose **No**, pick the currency you pay in. Your organization's owner can also answer this in **Settings → Organization**.
2. The final price, in the currency you'll pay in.
3. **Continue to payment** — once you've ticked the agreement box, saves your answer and opens the payment page. After your first payment the answer can only be changed through **Support**.

## Renewal, cancelling and refunds

Plans renew automatically at the end of each period. To stop the renewal, click **Cancel subscription** — you keep your plan until the end of the period you've paid for.

You can ask for a refund within 7 days of a payment. Approved refunds are sent within 14 business days, minus payment processing fees. If you bought a plan during your trial and get a full refund, your organization goes back to its trial for the days that are left. All the details are in our [Refund Policy](/refund).

Every payment, refund and failed payment is listed under **Payments** at the bottom of the Billing page. Click **Receipt** next to a payment to open its receipt, which you can print or save as PDF for your accounts. Payment records are kept for 5 years. If you don't recognise a charge, write to Support before contacting your bank.

## When the trial or plan ends

If the trial ends, or a paid plan ends without being renewed, HRHandle is paused until you choose a plan. Your data is kept. Owners and admins see the Billing page and can pay straight away; other teammates are asked to contact them. If a renewal payment fails, your plan stays available for 3 more days, so you have time to pay again.
