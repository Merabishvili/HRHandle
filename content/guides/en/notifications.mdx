HRHandle keeps your team up to date in three ways: notifications under the bell in the header, emails, and messages in your team's Slack or Microsoft Teams channel.

## Who is notified about what

| Notification | Who gets it |
|---|---|
| New application through an apply link | The owner and admins |
| You were @mentioned in a note | The person mentioned |
| Interview scheduled | The person who scheduled it and the interviewer |
| Interview reminder, the day before | The interviewer — or the owner and admins if nobody is assigned |
| Offer accepted or declined | The owner and admins |
| Candidate hired | The owner and admins |
| Candidate withdrew their application — with their reason, if they gave one | The owner and admins |
| A teammate was invited | The owner and admins, except whoever sent the invitation |
| The plan's candidate limit is reached | The owner and admins |
| AI Fit Analysis is ready | The person who asked for it |

Click the bell to see your notifications (see [Find your way around](/guide/navigation)). Each person decides in **Settings → Notifications** which ones appear under the bell and which also come by email — see [Your profile and security](/guide/profile-and-security). Emails go out straight away, one per event.

## Slack and Microsoft Teams

Send hiring news to a channel your team already reads. Owners and admins open **Settings → Integrations** and click **Configure →** next to Slack & Microsoft Teams notifications.

1. **Add webhook** — connect another channel. You can connect as many channels as you like.
2. **A connected channel** — **Test** posts a test message to it, **Disable** pauses it and **Delete** disconnects it.
3. **Events** — what the channel receives. Click **Edit events** to change them.

## Connect a channel

Click **Add webhook**.

<Screenshot
  name="slack-teams-add"
  alt="The form for adding a channel with the channel type, name, webhook URL and events highlighted"
  caption="Paste the channel's webhook URL and pick the events."
/>

1. **Channel** — **Slack** or **Microsoft Teams**.
2. **Name (e.g., #hiring)** — so you recognise the channel in this list.
3. **Webhook URL** — the channel's incoming-webhook address. The line under the field shows where to find it. In Slack it's an Incoming Webhooks app; in Teams, open the channel's **⋯** menu → **Workflows** and choose "Post to a channel when a webhook request is received".
4. **Events** — tick what to post: new applications, hires, rejections, withdrawals, offers sent, accepted and declined, and scheduled interviews.

Click **Save webhook**, then **Test** to check that the message arrives.

This is separate from the Microsoft connection in [Integrations](/guide/integrations): that one creates Teams *meetings* for interviews, this one posts *messages* to a channel.
