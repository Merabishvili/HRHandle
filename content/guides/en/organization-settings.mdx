**Settings → Organization** holds everything that applies to your whole organization: its name and logo, the address of your public jobs page, the language candidates see, security rules for your team, and AI Fit Analysis. Only the organization's **Owner** can open it.

## Name, logo and jobs page address

<Screenshot
  name="organization-settings"
  alt="The Organization card with the logo, organization name and public jobs page URL highlighted"
  caption="What candidates see as your company."
/>

1. **Organization logo** — a JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF image of up to 2 MB. It appears on your apply forms and your public jobs page.
2. **Organization name** — your company's name, shown to candidates and to your team.
3. **Public Jobs Page URL** — the address of the page that lists your open vacancies: `hrhandle.com/jobs/your-company`. Use lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. If you change it, links to the old address stop working.

Click **Save changes** when you're done.

## Security, language, AI and deletion

<Screenshot
  name="organization-policies"
  alt="The Two-factor authentication policy, Language, AI Fit Analysis and Danger zone cards"
  caption="Organization-wide rules."
/>

1. **Two-factor authentication policy** — make two-factor authentication mandatory: **Require 2FA for everyone**, or **Require 2FA for owners and admins only**. Anyone who hasn't set it up yet is asked to do so before they can continue using HRHandle. Click **Save policy**.
2. **Language** — the **Default language** of everything candidates see: job pages, apply forms, emails and offers. AI features also write in this language. It doesn't change your team's interface — everyone picks their own in their profile.
3. **AI Fit Analysis** — an optional AI assistant that checks how an application matches the vacancy's scorecard. It's off until you turn it on. See [AI features](/guide/ai-features).
4. **Danger zone** — **Delete organization…** schedules your organization and all its data for permanent deletion. You have 30 days to change your mind by emailing support@hrhandle.com; after that, the data is gone for good.
