Connect the tools your team already uses, in **Settings → Integrations**. Calendar and video-meeting accounts are personal: each person connects their own, and HRHandle then creates the meeting for every video interview they schedule. Your company's LinkedIn page, Slack or Teams channels and Calendly are connected once for the whole organization, by the owner or an admin.

## Connect your calendar and meetings

<Screenshot
  name="integrations"
  alt="The Integrations settings with the Google Calendar, Zoom and Microsoft connections highlighted"
  caption="Connect the accounts you use for interviews."
/>

1. **Google Calendar** — click **Connect Google** and allow access. Video interviews then get a Google Meet link and an event in your Google Calendar, with the candidate invited.
2. **Zoom** — click **Connect Zoom**. Video interviews get a Zoom meeting link.
3. **Microsoft (Calendar + Teams)** — click **Connect Microsoft**. Video interviews get a Teams meeting and an event in your Outlook calendar.

Once you're connected, the scheduling form lets you choose how to create the meeting — **Auto Meet**, **Auto Zoom**, **Auto Teams** or **Manual** for a link you paste yourself (see [Schedule interviews](/guide/schedule-interview)). If you connect more than one, **Default for video interviews** decides which one is used first.

When you cancel an interview, HRHandle also removes the meeting it created. To disconnect an account, click **Disconnect** next to it.

## Your company's LinkedIn page

Connect your company's LinkedIn page, and every vacancy gets a **Post to LinkedIn Jobs** button — see [Share a vacancy](/guide/share-a-vacancy).

<Screenshot
  name="integrations-linkedin"
  alt="The LinkedIn Company Page connection, connected, with its page ID and the Disconnect button highlighted"
  caption="One LinkedIn page for the whole organization."
/>

1. **LinkedIn Company Page** — owners and admins enter the page's numeric ID and click **Connect**. The ID is in the address of your page's admin view: linkedin.com/company/**ID**/admin/. Once it's connected, the page ID is shown here, and **Disconnect** removes it. Other teammates see whether the page is connected.

## Slack and Microsoft Teams notifications

To post hiring news to a Slack or Teams channel, owners and admins click **Configure →** under **Slack & Microsoft Teams notifications**. That's a separate connection for the whole organization — see [Notifications, Slack and Teams](/guide/notifications).

## Calendly

With Calendly, candidates pick their interview time themselves. Owners and admins click **Configure →** under **Calendly**, then **Connect Calendly**, and sign in to Calendly.

<Screenshot
  name="integrations-calendly"
  alt="The Calendly page, connected, with the connected account and the event type for candidate interviews highlighted"
  caption="Choose which Calendly event candidates book."
/>

1. **Connected as** — the Calendly account HRHandle uses. **Disconnect** removes the connection.
2. **Event type used for candidate interviews** — pick one of your Calendly event types and click **Save**. Candidates book this one.

After that, anyone on the team can send a candidate a booking link from their profile — see [Schedule interviews](/guide/schedule-interview).
