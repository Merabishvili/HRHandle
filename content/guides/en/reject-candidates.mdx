When a candidate isn't moving forward, reject them with a reason. The reason stays with the application, and you decide each time whether the candidate gets an email. You can reject from the candidate's profile (**Reject** under **Actions**), by dragging their card to **Rejected** on the pipeline, or with R in Review mode.

## Reject a candidate

<Screenshot
  name="reject-dialog"
  alt="The Reject candidate dialog with the rejection reason, the email switch, the email template and the confirm button highlighted"
  caption="A reason, and an email if you want one."
/>

1. **Rejection reason** — choose from your organization's list.
2. **Send rejection email** — off by default. Turn it on to email the candidate.
3. **Email template** — the template linked to the reason is picked for you, with a preview of the subject and text. Placeholders such as `{{candidate_name}}` and `{{role}}` are replaced with the candidate's name and the vacancy when the email is sent.
4. **Confirm Rejection** — rejects the candidate (and sends the email, if it's on).

The candidate leaves the active stages and appears among the closed outcomes on the pipeline, with the reason. If they have no other open applications, their status changes to **Inactive**. A sent offer is withdrawn, so it can no longer be accepted, and their upcoming interviews are cancelled. The rejection shows in their activity and in the audit log.

## Reject several candidates at once

On the [pipeline](/guide/pipeline), tick the candidates' cards and click **Reject** in the action bar.

<Screenshot
  name="reject-batch"
  alt="The dialog for rejecting two candidates with one reason and an email to each"
  caption="One reason and one email template for everyone you selected."
/>

The same reason applies to everyone. **Send rejection email to each candidate** is off by default, as for a single rejection. Turn it on and each of them gets their own personalised email.

## Reject everyone left when a vacancy closes

When a vacancy is filled or cancelled, close it out in one go. When owners and admins close or archive a vacancy that still has candidates in progress, HRHandle asks whether to reject them — see [The vacancy page](/guide/vacancy-page). The same window opens from **Reject remaining candidates** in the vacancy's **⋯** menu, and from the reminder a closed vacancy shows under **Needs your attention**.

<Screenshot
  name="reject-remaining"
  alt="The Reject remaining candidates window with the stages, an exception, the reason and email template, the email switch and the reject button highlighted"
  caption="Tick the stages; keep anyone back by name."
/>

1. **Stages** — every stage that still has candidates, with how many. The ticked stages are rejected. **Offer** starts unticked (**Not included by default**), so tick it only if you mean it.
2. **Exceptions** — search by name to keep someone in the pipeline. They're taken off their stage's count.
3. **Rejection reason** and **Email template** — one reason for everyone; the template linked to the reason is picked for you.
4. **Send rejection email** — off by default. Turn it on to send everyone their own email; **Preview email** shows it with the first candidate's name filled in. If someone has no email address, the window tells you.
5. **Reject** — rejects everyone in the ticked stages except the exceptions. Hired, rejected and withdrawn candidates are never changed.

Each of them moves to **Rejected** with the reason, as with a single rejection, and a summary shows how many were rejected and how many emails were sent.

## Rejection reasons

Owners and admins manage the list in **Settings → Rejection reasons**.

<Screenshot
  name="rejection-reasons"
  alt="The Rejection reasons settings with a reason and the field for adding a new one highlighted"
  caption="The reasons your team chooses from."
/>

1. **A reason** — **Template configured** means the reason has its own email template; otherwise the default rejection email is used. The pencil renames the reason and the bin deletes it.
2. **New reason** — type a name and click **Add**. You can have up to 50 reasons.

You write the email for each reason in **Settings → Email templates** — see [Email templates](/guide/candidate-emails).
