Once a vacancy is open, share it where candidates are looking. Every open vacancy can have an apply link, and all your public vacancies appear together on your organization's jobs page. Candidates don't need an account to apply.

## The apply link

Open the vacancy and go to the **Apply form** tab.

<Screenshot
  name="share-apply-link"
  alt="The Apply form tab with the screening questions, the active apply link and the Deactivate Form button"
  caption="The screening questions and the vacancy's apply link."
/>

The **Screening questions** at the top are the questions applicants answer on the form. You can add, change or remove them here — see [Create a vacancy](/guide/post-a-vacancy).

1. **Apply link** — the vacancy's public link. The buttons next to it copy the link or open the form. **What candidates will see** lists what's on the form: the job description; first name, last name, email, phone and LinkedIn fields; a CV upload (PDF or Word, required); and an automatic confirmation email after they apply.
2. **Deactivate Form** — the link stops working immediately. Candidates who have already applied stay.

If a vacancy has no link yet — for example because **Show on public jobs page** was off when you published it — click **Activate Application Form** on this tab.

The same link is behind **Copy apply link** in the vacancy's header and the **Share** box on the Overview tab.

## Where to share it

Paste the link wherever your candidates will see it:

- a LinkedIn post or a LinkedIn job listing (see below);
- job boards;
- your company website and social media;
- an email or a message to someone you'd like to apply.

Applications through the link arrive in the vacancy's first stage, **Applied**, marked as coming from the public form, and HRHandle notifies you. See [What candidates see](/guide/candidate-experience) for the candidate's side.

## Post it on LinkedIn Jobs

Once your company's LinkedIn page is connected (see [Integrations](/guide/integrations)), the **Share** box on the vacancy's **Overview** tab has a **Post to LinkedIn Jobs** button.

<Screenshot
  name="share-linkedin"
  alt="The Share box on a vacancy's Overview tab with the Post to LinkedIn Jobs button highlighted"
  caption="LinkedIn Jobs, one click from the vacancy."
/>

1. **Post to LinkedIn Jobs** — opens the vacancy's title and description, ready to copy into LinkedIn.

<Screenshot
  name="share-linkedin-dialog"
  alt="The Post to LinkedIn Jobs window with the job title, the description, the apply link and the Open LinkedIn Job Posting button highlighted"
  caption="What LinkedIn's job form asks for, ready to copy."
/>

LinkedIn doesn't let other apps fill in a free job post, so you paste each part into LinkedIn's form yourself:

1. **Step 1 — Job title** and **Step 2 — Description** — change them here if you like, then paste them into the matching steps on LinkedIn. The description already includes the responsibilities and requirements.
2. **Step 3 — Manage candidates** — copy the apply link. On LinkedIn's job settings step, find "Manage applicants", click **Edit**, choose **"On an external website"** and paste the link. Applicants then come to your HRHandle apply form instead of LinkedIn's.
3. **Open LinkedIn Job Posting** — opens LinkedIn's job form for your company page.

## Your public jobs page

Your organization has a public page that lists all your open vacancies. Its address is at the top of the **Vacancies** list.

<Screenshot
  name="share-jobs-page-link"
  alt="The Vacancies list with the public jobs page link highlighted"
  caption="Your public jobs page, one click from the Vacancies list."
/>

1. **Your public jobs page** — click the link to open it, or copy it to share all your vacancies at once.

<Screenshot
  name="share-jobs-page"
  alt="The public jobs page with three open vacancies and Apply links"
  caption="What job seekers see on your jobs page."
/>

The page shows your logo and company name, then every vacancy that is **Open** and has **Public** visibility, with its department, location, employment type and the start of its description. **Apply** opens the vacancy's apply page.

To list a vacancy here, turn on **Show on public jobs page** when you create or edit it. To keep a vacancy off the jobs page, turn that switch off: its apply link still works for the people you send it to. Change the page's address in [Organization settings](/guide/organization-settings).
