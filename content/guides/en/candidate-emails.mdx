HRHandle emails candidates at the key moments: when they apply, when you invite them to an interview, when they move forward, when you reject them and when you send an offer. Every email comes with sensible default text in your organization's language. Owners and admins can rewrite them in **Settings → Email templates**.

## Edit an email

<Screenshot
  name="email-templates"
  alt="The Email templates settings with the email tabs, the placeholders, the editor and the preview highlighted"
  caption="Pick an email, change its text and check the preview."
/>

1. **The emails** — **Application received**, **Interview invitation**, **Rejection**, **Status: Under review**, **Status: Interview** and **Offer sent**. Under the tabs, a sentence says when the selected email is sent.
2. **Placeholders** — words in double braces are filled in for each candidate when the email is sent: `{{candidate_name}}`, `{{role}}` (the vacancy), `{{company}}` and, depending on the email, the interview date and link or the offer link.
3. **Subject line** and **Message body** — write your own text. The greeting, the candidate's name and the footer are added automatically. Click **Save**; **Reset to default** brings back the original text.
4. **Preview** — the email as the candidate will see it, with sample details.

## Automatic stage emails

**Status: Under review** and **Status: Interview** go out automatically when you move a candidate forward to Screening or Interview. They're off until you switch them on.

<Screenshot
  name="email-templates-auto"
  alt="The Status: Under review email with its automatic sending switched off"
  caption="Switch an automatic email on when you want candidates to hear about every step."
/>

1. **Auto-email is off** / **Auto-email is on** — the switch for this email. Moving a candidate back to an earlier stage never sends it.

## Rejection emails

You can have a different rejection email for each rejection reason.

<Screenshot
  name="email-templates-rejection"
  alt="The Rejection tab with one template per rejection reason and the Add Template button highlighted"
  caption="One template per reason, chosen automatically when you reject."
/>

1. **A template** — its name and the reason it's linked to. Click it to see the text; the pencil edits it and the bin deletes it.
2. **Add Template** — give it a name, link it to a reason, and write the subject and text.

When you reject a candidate and choose a reason, HRHandle picks that reason's template for you — see [Reject candidates](/guide/reject-candidates).
