Getting started with HRHandle takes a couple of minutes. You create an account, confirm your email, and HRHandle sets up your organization with a free 7-day trial. You don't need a payment card.

## Step 1 — Create your account

Open [hrhandle.com](https://hrhandle.com) and click the sign-up button. Choose your language on the home page first: your account and your organization start in that language.

<Screenshot
  name="signup-form"
  alt="The Create your account page with the Google and Microsoft buttons and the sign-up form"
  caption="Sign up with Google, Microsoft, or your work email."
/>

1. **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** — sign up with your work account. You won't need a separate password.
2. Or fill in **Full name**, **Company name**, **Work email** and a **Password** of at least 8 characters, then click **Create account**.

By creating an account, you agree to the [Terms and Conditions](/terms) and the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Step 2 — Confirm your email

If you signed up with your email, HRHandle sends you a confirmation link.

<Screenshot
  name="signup-check-email"
  alt="The Check your email page shown after signing up"
  caption="Open the email and click the link to finish."
/>

The link works on any device and in any browser, so you can open it on your phone. Google and Microsoft sign-ups skip this step.

## Step 3 — Tell us about your company

If you signed up with Google or Microsoft, HRHandle asks two questions before you start.

<Screenshot
  name="onboarding-company"
  alt="The Finish setting up page with the Your name and Company name fields"
  caption="Google and Microsoft sign-ups add their name and company here."
/>

1. **Your name** — how your teammates will see you.
2. **Company name** — the name of your organization. Candidates see it too.
3. **Open portal** — creates your organization and opens HRHandle.

## Your first screen

You land on the **Pipeline**, HRHandle's home screen. It's empty until you create your first vacancy.

<Screenshot
  name="first-login"
  alt="The empty Pipeline of a new organization with the trial pill and the Create your first vacancy button highlighted"
  caption="A brand-new organization, ready for its first vacancy."
/>

1. **The trial pill** shows how many days of the trial are left. **Upgrade** opens your plans.
2. **Create your first vacancy** starts the vacancy wizard — see [Create a vacancy](/guide/post-a-vacancy). You can also **Import candidates** from a spreadsheet.

Behind the scenes, HRHandle has already:

1. Created your organization with the company name you entered.
2. Made you its **Owner**, the role with full access.
3. Started the 7-day trial.
4. Set your organization's language — the language candidates see — to the language you signed up in. You can change it in [Organization settings](/guide/organization-settings).

## What's included in the trial

Every feature works during the trial, including the AI features and the integrations. The only limits are:

| | Trial |
|---|---|
| Length | 7 days |
| Vacancies | 5 |
| Candidates | 100 |
| Team members | 2 |

When the trial ends, HRHandle is paused until you choose a plan. Your data is kept, so you can continue where you left off. See [Plans and billing](/guide/billing).

## Joining a colleague's organization

If a colleague invited you, don't create a new organization: open the link in the invitation email and sign up (or sign in) with the email address the invitation was sent to. You join their organization with the role they chose. See [Team and roles](/guide/team-and-roles).
