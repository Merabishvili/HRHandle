The pipeline is where candidates move from application to hire. Open **Pipeline** in the sidebar to see the candidates of every open vacancy on one board, with a column for each stage.

## The board

<Screenshot
  name="pipeline-board"
  alt="The Pipeline board with the role filters, the Board and List switch, Review new and a candidate card highlighted"
  caption="Every open vacancy on one board, one column per stage."
/>

1. **Role filters** — **All roles** shows everyone; click a vacancy to see only its candidates. The number is how many active candidates it has.
2. **Board** / **List** — switch between the columns and a table.
3. **Review new** — go through new applicants one by one (see below). The number shows how many are waiting.
4. **A candidate card** — the name and current job, how long the candidate has been in this stage and where they came from. **Fit 8.3** is the average of the team's assessments, out of 10 — see [Assessments and scorecards](/guide/assessments-and-scorecards). After 5 days in the same stage the card turns amber and says **stale**. Click the name to open the profile.

To move a candidate, drag the card to another column:

- Dropping it on **Rejected** opens the rejection dialog — see [Reject candidates](/guide/reject-candidates).
- Moving a candidate forward into **Screening** or **Interview** can also email them, if you've turned that email on — see [Email templates](/guide/candidate-emails).
- Moving a candidate to **Hired** notifies the owner and admins.

## Closed outcomes

Rejected and withdrawn candidates leave the stage columns. Click the narrow strip at the right end of the board to fold them out.

<Screenshot
  name="pipeline-closed"
  alt="The Closed group with two rejected candidates and their rejection reasons"
  caption="Who left the pipeline, why and when."
/>

Each card shows the rejection reason and how long ago it happened. Click **collapse** to fold the group away again.

## Act on several candidates at once

Tick the box on the cards you want to work with.

<Screenshot
  name="pipeline-bulk"
  alt="Two selected candidate cards and the bulk action bar at the bottom of the page"
  caption="Select candidates, then choose what to do with all of them."
/>

1. **Selected cards** — tick or untick the box on a card.
2. **The action bar** — **Move to stage** moves everyone to the stage you pick. **Schedule** books an interview (select exactly one candidate). **Email** opens your mail app with every selected candidate in BCC. **Reject** rejects them all in one go. **×** clears the selection.

## Review new applicants

**Review new** shows the applicants nobody has moved yet, one at a time, oldest first. It follows the role filter, so pick a vacancy first to review only its applicants.

<Screenshot
  name="pipeline-review"
  alt="Review mode with a new applicant, their CV, screening answers, the action buttons and the keyboard shortcuts highlighted"
  caption="Decide on each new applicant without leaving the keyboard."
/>

1. **Actions** — **Reject** (R), **Skip** (K), **Schedule** an interview (S), or **Advance** the candidate to the next stage (A).
2. **Open full profile** — leaves review mode and opens the candidate's profile.
3. **Shortcuts** — ← and → move between applicants; Esc closes review mode.

The card shows the vacancy, when and how the candidate applied, their CV (click it to open it in a new tab) and their answers to the screening questions. An answer that fails a knockout question is highlighted.

## Each vacancy's own board

Every vacancy also has its own board: open the vacancy and click **View pipeline →**. It works the same way, with that vacancy's own stages.

## Set up your stages

Owners and admins choose the stages in **Settings → Main pipeline**.

<Screenshot
  name="pipeline-main-settings"
  alt="Main pipeline settings with the list of stages and the Add stage button highlighted"
  caption="Your organization's default stages."
/>

1. **The stages** — in order. Drag a stage by its handle to reorder it, or click the bin to remove it.
2. **Add stage** — give the stage a name and choose its type. Up to 10 stages.

A stage's type decides what the candidate profile offers at that stage:

| Type | On the candidate profile |
|---|---|
| Standard | Move the candidate and add notes. |
| Review / Assessment | The screening checks: the candidate's answers to the screening questions, with failed knockout questions flagged. |
| Interview | Schedule an interview, fill in a scorecard, give feedback. |
| Offer | Create and track an offer. |

**Terminal** stages — Hired, Rejected and Withdrawn — take a candidate out of the active pipeline.

New vacancies start with these stages, and each vacancy can then change its own (see [The vacancy page](/guide/vacancy-page)). To give vacancies that have no applications yet the new set of stages, click **Apply to … empty vacancies**.
