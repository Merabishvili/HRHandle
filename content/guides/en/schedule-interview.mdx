Schedule interviews from the **Interviews** page, from a candidate's profile (**Schedule** under **Actions**), from the pipeline's action bar or with S in Review mode. Every interview, upcoming and past, is listed on the **Interviews** page.

## The Interviews page

Open **Interviews** in the sidebar.

<Screenshot
  name="interviews-list"
  alt="The Interviews page with the Schedule interview button, the filters and an upcoming interview highlighted"
  caption="Upcoming interviews first, grouped by day."
/>

1. **Schedule interview** — opens the scheduling form.
2. **Filters** — **All**, **Scheduled** (still to come), **Past** (the time has passed but nobody recorded how it went), **Cancelled** and **No show**. The counters above show the same numbers.
3. **An interview** — the candidate, vacancy and interviewer, the date and time, and the status. **Join** opens the meeting link. Interviews are grouped into today, tomorrow, this week, later and past.

An interview whose time has passed without an outcome says **Past due — mark complete or no-show**, so it doesn't stay open forever.

## Schedule an interview

<Screenshot
  name="interview-form"
  alt="The Schedule interview form with the candidate, interview type, date and time, interviewer, meeting link and summary highlighted"
  caption="Everything about the interview on one page, with a summary on the right."
/>

1. **Candidate** — when you start from a candidate, the candidate and the vacancy are filled in. Otherwise choose the candidate, then one of the vacancies they're being considered for.
2. **Interview type** — **Video**, **Phone** or **On-site**.
3. **Date**, **Time** and **Duration** — HRHandle suggests the next slot in working hours.
4. **Interviewer** — the teammate who runs the interview.
5. **Meeting link (optional)** — for a video interview, paste a Zoom, Teams or Google Meet link. If you've connected Google Calendar, Zoom or Microsoft Teams, you can let HRHandle create the meeting instead — see [Integrations](/guide/integrations).
6. **Summary** — check the details. Tick **Email candidate** to send the candidate an invitation with the date, time and link, then click **Schedule**.

You and the interviewer get a notification, and the interviewer gets a reminder the day before. The interview also appears on the candidate's stage card, with **Join** and **Reschedule**.

## Let the candidate pick a time with Calendly

If your organization has connected Calendly (see [Integrations](/guide/integrations)), the candidate's profile has a **Calendly link** button under **Actions**.

<Screenshot
  name="calendly-link"
  alt="The Actions section of a candidate's profile with the Calendly link button highlighted"
  caption="A booking link for this candidate, next to Schedule."
/>

1. **Calendly link** — creates the candidate's booking link for this vacancy.

<Screenshot
  name="calendly-link-dialog"
  alt="The Calendly scheduling link window with the link and the Copy link button"
  caption="Copy the link and send it to the candidate."
/>

Click **Copy link** and send the link to the candidate in your own email or message. When they pick a time, the interview appears on their profile and on the **Interviews** page by itself. If they cancel in Calendly, the interview is cancelled in HRHandle as well.

## Reschedule, cancel or record the outcome

Click **⋯** next to an interview on the **Interviews** page.

<Screenshot
  name="interview-actions"
  alt="An interview's menu with Reschedule interview, Mark complete, Cancel interview and Mark as no show"
  caption="Keep each interview's status up to date."
/>

- **Reschedule interview** — pick a new date, time and duration, and choose whether to email the candidate the new time.
- **Mark complete** — the interview took place.
- **Cancel interview** — it won't take place. A meeting that HRHandle created in Google Calendar or Zoom is removed too.
- **Mark as no show** — the candidate didn't turn up.

After the interview, record what you thought of the candidate in their assessment — see [Assessments and scorecards](/guide/assessments-and-scorecards).
