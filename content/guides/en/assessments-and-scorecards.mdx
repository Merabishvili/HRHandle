An assessment is one interviewer's verdict on a candidate: notes on the interview questions, a rating from 1 to 5 for each skill on the vacancy's scorecard, and an overall recommendation. Every interviewer fills in their own, and the candidate's profile keeps them all.

The scorecard and the interview questions belong to the vacancy. You set them when you create it, and can change them any time in the vacancy's **Scorecard & interview** tab — see [Create a vacancy](/guide/post-a-vacancy) and [The vacancy page](/guide/vacancy-page).

## Open the assessment

When a candidate is at the interview stage, the stage card on their profile has the scorecard button.

<Screenshot
  name="assessment-open"
  alt="The stage card at the interview stage with the Edit scorecard button highlighted"
  caption="Your scorecard lives on the candidate's stage card."
/>

1. **Add full scorecard** — opens the assessment. After you've submitted it, the button reads **Edit scorecard**, and **Your scorecard** above it shows your recommendation, fit and reason.

## Fill in the assessment

The assessment opens on the right, in three parts.

<Screenshot
  name="assessment-sheet"
  alt="The assessment with the questions, the scorecard ratings, the conclusion and the Submit button highlighted"
  caption="Answers, ratings and a recommendation in one place."
/>

1. **Questions** — the vacancy's interview questions, with room to write down the candidate's answers.
2. **Scorecard** — rate each skill from 1 (**Below**) through 3 (**Meets**) to 5 (**Exceeds**). **Must-have** skills are marked. The **Average score** updates as you go; click a rating again to clear it.
3. **Conclusion** — pick an **Overall recommendation** (**Strong yes**, **Yes**, **Lean no** or **No**) and add a one-line reason.
4. **Submit** — finishes your assessment.

Every field is optional, and everything you type is saved automatically, so you can close the assessment and come back to it later.

To keep the team from influencing each other, the other interviewers' assessments stay hidden here until you submit yours. After that, they appear under **Other scorecards** with their recommendation and fit.

## The team's assessments

Every submitted assessment stays on the candidate's profile under **Assessments** — also after the candidate is hired or rejected. The owner and admins see all of them. Other teammates see a colleague's assessment once they've submitted their own for the same vacancy.

<Screenshot
  name="assessment-record"
  alt="The Assessments section with one assessment opened, showing the criteria ratings, the answers and the recommendation"
  caption="Click an assessment to see it in full."
/>

1. **An assessment** — the average rating out of 5, the vacancy, the recommendation, who assessed and when. Click it to see each rating, the answers and the reason.

## Share an assessment

Owners and admins can share one assessment with someone who isn't in HRHandle — for example a hiring manager at a partner company. Open the assessment under **Assessments** and click **Share** at the bottom.

<Screenshot
  name="assessment-share"
  alt="An opened assessment with the Share button highlighted"
  caption="Each interviewer's assessment is shared on its own."
/>

1. **Share** — opens the sharing window for this assessment.

<Screenshot
  name="assessment-share-dialog"
  alt="The Share assessment window with the share link, the Copy button and Revoke link highlighted"
  caption="A private link to this one assessment."
/>

1. **Share link** — click **Generate link** the first time, then **Copy** and send the link. Anyone with the link can open the assessment, without an account.
2. **Revoke link** — the link stops working at once. You can generate a new one later.

Once shared, the assessment is marked **Shared** in the list.

<Screenshot
  name="assessment-shared-page"
  alt="The shared assessment page with the candidate, the vacancy, the average score, the assessor, the criteria ratings, the answers and the recommendation"
  caption="What the person you share it with sees, in your organization's language."
/>

The page shows the candidate's name, the vacancy and your company, the average score, who assessed and when, each rating, the answers and the recommendation with its reason. It doesn't show contact details, notes or the candidate's stage.

## How the fit score is calculated

Once every skill is rated, the assessment gets a fit percentage: the sum of the ratings divided by the highest possible sum. Ratings of 4, 4, 5 and 3, for example, give 16 out of 20 — 80%. The card on the [pipeline](/guide/pipeline) shows the average of the submitted assessments on a 10-point scale: assessments of 80% and 85% show as **Fit 8.3**.
