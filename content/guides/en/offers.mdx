When you've chosen your candidate, send them the offer from HRHandle. The candidate gets a private link where they can read it and accept or decline, and you see their answer straight away. Owners and admins create and send offers; other teammates see the offer and its status on the candidate's profile.

## Create an offer

Move the candidate to the **Offer** stage. Their stage card then turns into the offer form.

<Screenshot
  name="offer-create"
  alt="The Create offer form with compensation and currency, the dates, the offer details, the personal note and the save buttons highlighted"
  caption="The candidate sees everything you fill in here. Compensation and dates are optional."
/>

1. **Compensation** and **Currency** — for example 6500 and GEL.
2. **Start date** and **Respond-by date** — when the job starts and how long the offer stays open. After the respond-by date the offer expires and can no longer be accepted.
3. **Offer details** — the offer itself, in your own words: the role, team, salary, benefits.
4. **Personal note to the candidate (optional)** — a few words of your own. The offer page shows it as **A note from the recruiter**.
5. **Save draft** keeps the offer to finish later. **Save & send** emails the candidate a link to the offer.

## Follow the offer

Once the offer is sent, the stage card shows it with its status.

<Screenshot
  name="offer-sent"
  alt="A sent offer with Copy offer link, Withdraw and Mark as Hired highlighted"
  caption="The offer's status, its link, and the final step: the hire."
/>

1. **Copy offer link** — copy the link to send it another way, for example in a chat.
2. **Withdraw** — take the offer back. The candidate's page then says it was withdrawn, and you can create a new offer.
3. **Mark as Hired** — hire the candidate. You don't have to wait for them to accept.

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| Draft | Saved, not sent yet. |
| Sent | Waiting for the candidate's answer. |
| Accepted | The candidate accepted. |
| Declined | The candidate declined, optionally with a reason. |
| Expired | The respond-by date passed without an answer. |
| Withdrawn | You took the offer back. |

When the candidate accepts or declines, the owner and admins get a notification. If they decline with a reason, the reason is in the notification and under the declined offer in **Previous offers** on the stage card. An accepted offer doesn't hire the candidate by itself: the stage card shows **Offer accepted** and a **Mark as Hired** button, so you finish the hire when you're ready.

## What the candidate sees

<Screenshot
  name="offer-page"
  alt="The candidate's offer page with the role, compensation, dates, offer details, the recruiter's note and the Decline and Accept offer buttons"
  caption="The offer page, in your organization's language."
/>

The page shows everything from the offer form, with your personal note under **A note from the recruiter**.

1. **Decline** / **Accept offer** — the candidate answers right on the page. They're asked to confirm, and when declining they can add a short reason.

Under the offer, **Not sure?** lets the candidate email the person who sent it. More about the pages candidates see in [What candidates see](/guide/candidate-experience).
