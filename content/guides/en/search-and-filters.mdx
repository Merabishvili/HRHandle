Two tools help you find what you need: the global search, which looks everywhere at once, and the search and filters on the candidate and vacancy lists.

## Search everything

Click **Search…** in the header, or press Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) or the / key from anywhere in HRHandle.

<Screenshot
  name="search-palette"
  alt="The search window with a candidate found by first name"
  caption="Results appear as you type, grouped by type."
/>

Type a name, an email, a vacancy title or a word from a note. Results are grouped into **Candidates**, **Vacancies** and **Notes**; click one, or use the arrow keys and Enter, to open it. Press Esc to close the search.

## Search and filter the candidate list

From the sidebar, click **Candidates**.

<Screenshot
  name="candidates-list-tools"
  alt="The Candidates list with the search box, status tabs, sort and columns, and the export and import buttons highlighted"
  caption="Narrow the list down, choose what it shows, and get your data out."
/>

1. **Search candidates…** — matches names and emails.
2. **Status tabs** — show **All** candidates, or only those who are **Active**, **Inactive** or **Hired**.
3. **Sort** and **Columns** — sort the list (for example newest first), and choose which columns to show.
4. **Export CSV** downloads all your candidates as a spreadsheet file. **Bulk import** and **Add candidate** bring new people in — see [Import candidates](/guide/import-candidates) and [Add a candidate](/guide/add-candidates).

The **Vacancies** list has the same tools: a search box, status tabs (**All**, **Draft**, **Open**, **On hold**, **Closed**, **Archived**), sorting and columns.

## Choose your columns

Click **Columns** to decide what the list shows.

<Screenshot
  name="columns-dialog"
  alt="The column settings window with columns that can be turned on or off"
  caption="Turn columns on or off. Your choice is saved for you."
/>

Turn on the columns you need — including your organization's custom fields — turn off the rest, and drag columns to change their order. Each person's choice is saved separately, so changing it doesn't affect your teammates.
