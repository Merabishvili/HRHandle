Every vacancy has its own page that brings everything about the role together. Open it from **Vacancies** by clicking the vacancy's title. At the top are the main actions; below them are five tabs. **Previous vacancy** and **Next vacancy** move between vacancies without going back to the list.

## Overview

<Screenshot
  name="vacancy-overview"
  alt="The Overview tab of a vacancy with the header actions, tabs, the attention list, the pipeline summary and At a glance highlighted"
  caption="The Overview tab: what needs doing and how the vacancy is going."
/>

1. **Header actions** — **Copy apply link** copies the link candidates use to apply. **View pipeline →** opens this vacancy's board. **⋯** holds more actions (see below).
2. **The tabs** — Overview, Job description, Scorecard & interview, Apply form and Settings.
3. **Needs your attention** — what's waiting for you on this vacancy: a sent offer the candidate hasn't answered yet, a candidate at the **Offer** stage whose offer isn't created or sent yet, an interview in the next day and a half, and new applicants to review. Each item has a button that takes you there. When nothing is waiting, HRHandle tells you so.
4. **Pipeline** — how many candidates are in each stage. **Open pipeline →** opens the board.
5. **At a glance** — how long the vacancy has been open, the number of active candidates, the salary range, the end date and its **Health**.

| Health | Means |
|---|---|
| Good | A candidate moved to another stage in the last 7 days. |
| Watch | Nothing has moved for a week. |
| Stale | Nothing has moved for a week and the vacancy has been open for more than 30 days. |

Below that are the **Hiring team** — the hiring manager and the recruiter — and **Share**, with the apply link and a copy button. Once your company's LinkedIn page is connected, **Share** also has **Post to LinkedIn Jobs** — see [Share a vacancy](/guide/share-a-vacancy).

## More actions

Click **⋯** in the header.

<Screenshot
  name="vacancy-actions-menu"
  alt="The vacancy's menu with Edit vacancy, Reject remaining candidates, Duplicate vacancy and Delete"
  caption="Edit, duplicate or delete the vacancy."
/>

- **Edit vacancy** — opens the full form, with every field from the [Create vacancy](/guide/post-a-vacancy) wizard.
- **Reject remaining candidates** — owners and admins reject everyone still in progress in one go, for example once the vacancy is filled. See [Reject candidates](/guide/reject-candidates).
- **Duplicate vacancy** — creates a draft copy with the same details, scorecard and questions. Handy for similar roles.
- **Delete** — moves the vacancy to the Trash, where owners and admins can restore it for 30 days. See [Audit log and trash](/guide/audit-log-and-trash).

## Job description

<Screenshot
  name="vacancy-jd"
  alt="The Job description tab with Improve with AI and Edit, Posting details and Preview apply page highlighted"
  caption="The description candidates read, and how it's published."
/>

1. **Improve with AI** opens the edit form at the AI writer; **Edit** opens the form at the top.
2. **Posting details** — **Visibility** is Public when the vacancy is listed on your jobs page; the content **Language** and when it was last edited. Your organization's custom fields for vacancies appear below it.
3. **Preview apply page** — opens the public apply page exactly as candidates see it.

## Scorecard & interview

This tab is for your team only — candidates never see it.

<Screenshot
  name="vacancy-scorecard"
  alt="The Scorecard and Interview guide sections of the Scorecard & interview tab"
  caption="What interviewers rate, and what they ask."
/>

1. **Scorecard** — the attributes every interviewer rates from 1 to 5. Mark each as **Must-have** or **Nice-to-have**, and add more in the field at the bottom.
2. **Interview guide** — open-ended questions your interviewers ask in the interview.

**Suggest with AI**, above them, drafts attributes and questions from the job description; you add the ones you like one by one. More in [Assessments and scorecards](/guide/assessments-and-scorecards).

## Apply form

The **Apply form** tab holds the screening questions applicants answer and the vacancy's apply link. See [Share a vacancy](/guide/share-a-vacancy).

## Settings

<Screenshot
  name="vacancy-settings"
  alt="The Settings tab with Vacancy details, Status & visibility, This vacancy's pipeline and Danger zone highlighted"
  caption="The vacancy's details, status and stages."
/>

1. **Vacancy details** — title, department, location, employment type, work mode and end date. **Edit role details** opens the form.
2. **Status & visibility** — change the vacancy's status (see the table below).
3. **This vacancy's pipeline** — the stages candidates move through. They're copied from your Main pipeline when the vacancy is created; here you can rename, reorder, add or remove stages for this vacancy only. See [The pipeline](/guide/pipeline).
4. **Danger zone** — **Delete** moves the vacancy to the Trash.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Draft | Only your team sees the vacancy. Candidates can't apply. |
| Open | The vacancy accepts applications. It's listed on your jobs page if its visibility is Public. |
| On hold | Paused: the apply link stops accepting applications. Candidates already in the pipeline stay. |
| Closed | Finished: no new applications. The vacancy and its candidates stay. |
| Archived | Hidden from the active vacancy list and kept for your records. |
