Connect a LinkedIn company page to HRHandle and post a vacancy to LinkedIn Jobs in a few clicks. HRHandle prepares the title, description, and a public apply URL pointing back at your form; you paste them into LinkedIn's wizard and submit.

This integration uses LinkedIn's **free job posting** flow — no LinkedIn Recruiter subscription required.

## Connect your company page

Open **Settings → Integrations** and find the **LinkedIn Company Page** row. You need your numeric LinkedIn company page ID — not the slug. Find it in your LinkedIn company admin URL:

```
linkedin.com/company/12345678/admin/  ← the number is your page ID
```

Paste the ID, click **Connect**, and you are done. Nothing more is needed — no OAuth dance, no API keys.

## Post a vacancy to LinkedIn

Open any vacancy. With LinkedIn connected, a **Post to LinkedIn Jobs** button appears next to the other actions in the top-right.

## Use the clipboard companion modal

Clicking **Post to LinkedIn Jobs** opens a modal — your companion for filling out LinkedIn's wizard. LinkedIn's free flow does not accept URL parameters, so the modal gives you each piece of data ready to copy.

<Screenshot
  src="/guide/screenshots/linkedin-integration-modal.png"
  alt="The Post to LinkedIn Jobs modal showing editable title and description, the external apply URL with copy button, and an Open LinkedIn Job Posting button"
  caption="Edit the title and description if you want to refine them before pasting."
/>

What's in the modal:

1. **Step 1 — Job title**: pre-filled from the vacancy. Edit if you want.
2. **Step 2 — Description**: combined `Description + Responsibilities + Requirements`. Edit if you want.
3. **Step 3 — Manage applicants**: the public apply URL, plus instructions for the "On an external website" toggle on LinkedIn so candidates land on your HRHandle apply form.

Click **Open LinkedIn Job Posting** at the bottom. A new tab opens at LinkedIn's job creation wizard, scoped to your company. Switch back to the HRHandle tab to copy each field as you progress through LinkedIn's steps.

## Why isn't it fully automatic?

LinkedIn's free job posting flow doesn't accept pre-fill URL parameters or expose an unauthenticated API for posting. The official Job Posting API requires LinkedIn's **ATS Partner Program** approval (a months-long vetting). For free-tier users, copy-paste is the only path — but the modal makes it a 30-second job.

If your company has **LinkedIn Recruiter**, you get a richer job creation form at a different URL with extra targeting features. Our button still works for Recruiter users and lands them on the free flow; we don't auto-detect Recruiter access yet.

## Disconnect

To stop showing the button on vacancies, open **Settings → Integrations** and click **Disconnect** in the LinkedIn row. The integration record is removed; you can re-connect at any time by pasting the page ID again.
