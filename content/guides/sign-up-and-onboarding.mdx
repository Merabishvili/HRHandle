Creating an HRHandle organization is a one-screen sign-up plus an email confirmation. No setup wizard, no credit card. Your organization, profile, and 7-day trial are provisioned the first time you load the dashboard.

## Step 1 — Fill the sign-up form

Open [hrhandle.com](https://hrhandle.com) and click **Sign Up**, or go directly to `/auth/sign-up`. Enter your full name, company name, work email, and a password of at least 8 characters.

You can also sign up with **Google** or **Microsoft** if your work email is on one of those providers — the OAuth flow gets you straight to the confirmation step without needing a password.

## Step 2 — Confirm your email

After submitting, HRHandle sends you a confirmation link. You stay on a "Check your email" screen until you click the link.

<Screenshot
  src="/guide/screenshots/sign-up-and-onboarding-confirm.png"
  alt="The post sign-up screen instructing the user to check their email for a confirmation link"
  caption="Open the confirmation email and click the link — it works from any browser."
/>

The link uses HRHandle's `token_hash` flow, so you can open the email in a different browser or on your phone — it doesn't have to be the same browser you signed up in.

## Step 3 — Your organization is created automatically

Click the confirmation link. You land on `/dashboard`. Behind the scenes, the first dashboard load does three things at once:

1. **Creates your organization** with the company name you entered.
2. **Creates your profile** with the role **Owner** — the highest level of access.
3. **Starts a 7-day Trial subscription** with seat and feature limits suitable for evaluation.

You don't see these steps as a wizard — by the time the dashboard renders, everything is already provisioned. You're free to start posting vacancies, adding candidates, and inviting teammates immediately.

## What's included in the trial

| Limit | Trial |
|---|---|
| Vacancies | 5 |
| Candidates | 100 |
| Team seats | 2 (you + 1 invitee) |
| Days | 7 from sign-up |

You can use every feature during the trial — public apply forms, pipeline, custom fields, integrations, email templates, all of it. If you hit a limit before deciding, upgrade in **Settings → Subscription**.

## After the trial

When the trial expires, the dashboard redirects you to **Subscription** until a paid plan is chosen. Your data is never deleted — pick a plan and pick up where you left off.
